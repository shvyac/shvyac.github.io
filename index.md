# shvyac — Awesome Lists

## English

Hi, I'm **shvyac**. I work at the crossroads of **CAE (computer-aided engineering)**,
**AI-driven engineering**, and **IT / data infrastructure**. With a long background in
automotive CAE (structural FEA, NVH, crash simulation), I now spend my time exploring how
AI, HPC, and open-source tools can reshape engineering work.

This site is my personal knowledge hub: a collection of **self-made awesome lists** that I
curate and refresh every week. Each list is built from tools I actually star, test, and
follow — not just copy-pasted link dumps.

**What you'll find here**

- **CAE / CAD / Engineering** — CAD/CAE resources, meshless CAE, open-source solvers, AI tools for mechanical engineering
- **AI & Automation** — local LLMs, AI × OSINT, AI × CMS, AI × Redmine, AI × requirements engineering
- **Security, Cloud & Governance** — ISO 27001 / ISMS tools, Azure AI / Dynamics 365 / Power Platform security
- **Data, Dashboards & Maps** — Grafana-style dashboards, AI executive dashboards, maps / GIS
- **Finance** — stock, market, and trading resources
- **Hobbies** — ham radio, ballroom dance, audio software, M5Stack, AI "ghost" artists, online services

Every list comes in English, and many also in Japanese (`README.ja.md`).
Stars, issues, and suggestions are always welcome.
